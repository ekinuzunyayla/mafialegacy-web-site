Privacy Policy

Mafia Legacy: Gangster Wars · Last updated 18 October 2026

The short version

  • You can play without signing up. The game creates an anonymous account to keep your game on our server.
  • If you link Apple or Google, we keep that link so you can open your game on another device.
  • Purchases go through Apple or Google. We never see your card or payment details.
  • Ads are rewarded only, and only when you choose to watch one. Where the law asks for consent, you are asked first.
  • You can delete your account and its game at any time, in the app or on the web.

1. Who is responsible

Mafia Legacy is published by Ekin Uzunyayla, an individual developer based in Türkiye, who is the data controller for the data described here (“we”, “us”). Contact: ekinnuzunyayla@gmail.com.

2. Your account and your game

When you first open the app it signs in anonymously. On our server (Supabase, hosted in the European Union, Frankfurt) we keep:

  • A random account ID and when it was created and last used.
  • Your game: the name you chose for your boss, your progress, items, money, settings and the like. The game’s rules run on our server, so your game lives there.
  • If you link an account: that it is Apple or Google, and the identifier and email address that Apple or Google gives us for it. With Sign in with Apple you may choose to hide your email; we then receive a relay address.
  • The language the app uses, so other players’ names and messages to you can be shown in it.

3. Playing with others

Other players can see your boss’s name, portrait, level, power, title and gang. If you join a gang, its members see the lines you write in the gang chat; chat lines are kept for 30 days. If you report or block a player, we keep the report (who, why, and the line it was about) so that we can review it. Names and chat lines pass a word filter.

4. Purchases

Gold packs, the starter pack and the Consigliere Card are sold by the App Store or Google Play. RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA) handles the receipts for us. We receive which product you bought, when, and a transaction identifier, and use them to add what you bought to your game exactly once. We never receive your name, card number or billing address. A subscription is managed and cancelled in the store’s own settings.

5. Rewarded ads

You may choose to watch an ad for a reward (a little energy, or one more try at a safe). Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). AdMob may use your device’s advertising identifier and other device information to show and measure ads, and, where you allow it, to personalise them. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent with Google’s consent form before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice in the app’s Settings (“Privacy choices”). On iPhone, the system asks whether the app may track you; saying no only makes ads non-personalised. When you finish an ad, AdMob tells our server your account ID and which reward it was, so that we can give the reward; nothing else of the ad reaches us.

6. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app reminds you on your phone when your energy or stamina is full; these reminders are scheduled on the device. When another player raids you, our server may send a push through Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google LLC); for this we keep your device’s push token, whether you want raid notices, and your clock’s offset from UTC so we stay quiet at night. You can switch notices off in the app or in your phone’s settings.

7. Crash reports and game measurements

If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC) receives a crash report with the device model, system version and the state of the app at the crash. On our server we record game events such as reaching a level, a fight’s result, or gold earned and spent, tied to your account ID, to balance the game and to detect cheating. We do not use a separate analytics or advertising tracker of our own.

8. What we never collect

We do not ask for or collect your phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment details.

10. Who we share data with

Only with the service providers named above, each for its own part: Supabase (servers and database), RevenueCat (purchases), Apple and Google (sign-in, stores, push), Google AdMob (ads) and Firebase (crash reports, push). We do not sell your data.

11. International transfers

Our server is in the European Union. Some providers (RevenueCat, Google, Apple) may process data in the United States or elsewhere, under their standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

12. How long we keep data

  • Your account and game: until you delete them. A guest game that was never linked to Apple or Google is deleted 180 days after it was last used (a guest who made a purchase is kept).
  • Gang chat lines: 30 days. Game measurements: 180 days.
  • Purchase records: as long as your account exists.

13. Deleting your data

In the app: Settings › Account › Delete my account. This deletes your account, your game, your purchases’ records, your push tokens and your place in a gang; your chat lines stay without your name, and reports you sent stay without your name. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke the app’s access to your Apple account. On the web you can ask the same at our account deletion page. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in the store first.

14. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Türkiye’s KVKK), you may ask to access, correct, delete or receive your data, object to or restrict its use, and withdraw consent. Write to us at the address above. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

15. Children

The game is not meant for children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect data from them; if you believe a child has used the game, write to us and we will delete the account.

16. Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Our database is closed to the app: only our server’s own functions read and write it.

17. Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
